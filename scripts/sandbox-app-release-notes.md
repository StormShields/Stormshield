Try Storm Shield on your own computer: nothing to install, and no terminal. Everything you see is **SIMULATED**: a made-up Mesh of Sensors replaying a real flood.

## Download

- **Mac (Apple Silicon: M1 or newer):** `Storm-Shield-Sandbox-macos-arm64.zip`
- **Windows (64-bit):** `Storm-Shield-Sandbox-windows-x64.zip`

Unzip it, then open **Storm Shield Sandbox**. Your browser opens the dashboard. Press **Stop** in the Sandbox Panel when you are done; it also ends by itself ten minutes after you close the tab. It is reachable only from your own computer.

## The first time, your computer will warn you

The app is not signed (signing needs paid accounts), so Windows and macOS ask you to confirm. It is safe to continue, and the source of everything in it is in this repository.

**Windows:** if "Windows protected your PC" appears, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Mac:** if it says the app "can't be opened", close that message, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the message about Storm Shield Sandbox and click **Open Anyway**, then confirm.

If nothing seems to happen, look for `storm-shield-sandbox-failed.html` or `storm-shield-sandbox.log` in your temporary folder; the app opens a page saying what went wrong when it cannot start.
